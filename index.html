E-waste

Your old phone isn't just trash.

E-waste is any discarded device with a plug, battery or circuit board. Inside are useful materials worth recovering and substances that can harm people and nature if handled badly.

  • Why it matters: electronics contain metals that can be recovered and reused, saving mining and energy.
  • Why not the regular bin: batteries and circuit parts can leak toxins, start fires and contaminate soil and water.
  • Use authorized channels: take-back programs and authorized collection or recycling facilities are equipped to handle devices safely.
  • Protect your privacy: phones, laptops and tablets store personal data. Back up and wipe them before you let go.
